Project Overview

VF Corporation is one of the world's largest apparel, footwear, and accessories companies, owning iconic brands like Kipling and Eastpak. This project involved developing and expanding multi-country B2B and B2C storefront systems to support global operations, localized catalogs, regional currencies, and high transaction volumes.

Key Architecture Decisions

  • • Multi-Store Scoping: Constructed dynamic scoping rules for store configurations, allowing the division of branding elements and logic across Kipling and Eastpak instances while maintaining a unified admin architecture.
  • • CI/CD Optimization: Led the optimization of Jenkins pipelines and Docker environments to build stable release loops, enabling zero-downtime hotfixes and quick deployments.
  • • Search Fine-tuning: Configured advanced ElasticSearch indexing rules to handle complex category hierarchies and customized product option attributes.
